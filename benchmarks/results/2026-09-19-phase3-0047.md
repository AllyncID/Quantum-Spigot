# Phase 3 patch 0047 result

## Change

`ServerWaypointManager.updateWaypoint` skipped the missing-player scan when
the waypoint connection column was complete. A partial column kept the
original recovery loop.

## Matched local diagnostic

Windows 11, i3-12100F (8 logical processors), JDK 25, no plugins, pooled
Netty, locator and waypoint collections enabled, view distance 8, simulation
distance 6, pregenerated baseline spread fixture, same-host loopback bots, and
test-only `allow-flight=true` to avoid a flying kick hiding the tick window.

| Run | Jar | Players | Last rolling window | JFR top relevant methods |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Control | `5913d4b9...` (before 0047) | 300 | 6.17 TPS, 161.86 ms mean, p95 210.23 ms, p99 240.70 ms | `getNearestPlayer` 9.20%; `updateWaypoint` 2.06%; azimuth update 1.20% |
| Candidate | `097fb81e...` (0047) | 300 | 5.33–6.30 TPS, 161.86–200.94 ms mean, p95 210.23–292.81 ms, p99 240.70–443.73 ms | `getBukkitEntity` 10.29%; `updateWaypoint` 1.75%; azimuth update 1.15% |

Both runs stopped at the health gate before a valid sustained 20-TPS stage.
The candidate has no defensible MSPT or slope improvement on this matched
fixture. The small locator share change is not enough to offset the run-level
variance and the same dominant map/player work remains.

## Decision

**REVERT.** Patch 0047 is removed. No gameplay rule, tracking range, spawn
rule, or locator behavior is retained from this experiment.

The next candidate is the measured `NaturalSpawner` player lookup, using the
existing Moonrise `NearbyPlayers` index with an exact-predicate fallback.
